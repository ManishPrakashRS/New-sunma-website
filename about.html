Our Story

Woven in Nagercoil, cast across the world.

Sunma Fishnet started as a small mesh-weaving unit in Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu — a coastal district where fishing isn't an industry, it's daily life. We built our first looms to serve the boats we could see from our own workshop.

Today the same principle holds: a net is only as good as the water it's tested in. Every batch we weave is sized, dyed, and tension-tested for the specific fleet, farm, or job site it's headed to — not built to a generic shelf standard.

We manufacture HDPE, nylon, and polyester netting for trawling, gillnetting, aquaculture cages, and safety barriers, and ship both domestically and to export partners abroad.

Timeline

How the unit grew

FOUNDING

A workshop near the harbor

Started with a handful of knotting machines, weaving repair mesh and small nets for local trawler crews in and around Nagercoil.

EXPANSION

Raschel knotless looms added

Introduced knotless HDPE weaving to serve deep-sea trawling fleets needing lower-drag, higher-density mesh.

DIVERSIFICATION

Aquaculture and safety netting

Added UV-stabilized cage mesh and construction safety netting as fish farming and infrastructure demand grew across Tamil Nadu.

TODAY

Domestic supply and export

Now supplying fishing cooperatives, aquaculture farms, and contractors pan-India, with mesh shipped to export partners abroad.

What Guides Us

Three things every batch has to pass

01

Tested, not assumed

Every batch is pull-tested against its rated breaking strength before it leaves the unit — strength isn't a marketing number here.

02

Sized to the job

Mesh size, GSM, and ply are matched to the actual catch, cage, or load — we'd rather ask a question than ship the wrong gauge.

03

Built to outlast the season

UV stabilization and reinforced edges are standard wherever nets face long-term sun and saltwater exposure, not an upsell.
