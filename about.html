About SUNMA Fishnet

Manufacturer & supplier of nylon fishing nets.

SUNMA Fishnet manufactures durable nylon monofilament and multi fishing nets for commercial fishing fleets, fish traders, marine distributors, and aquaculture-related buyers — based in Nagercoil, Tamil Nadu.

Factory / Harbour Photo
Our Story

Manufactured for the buyers who actually focussd on Fishing Nets and Agriculture & Aquaculture Nets

SUNMA Fishnet is built around one focus: tight, slip-free knots that hold up under real fishing conditions. We manufacture nylon monofilament and multi nets using Japan-made Amita DK machines and Indian BMW machines, giving fishing fleets and traders consistent knot quality order after order.

From single-boat orders to Export distributor-scale bulk supply, every net is produced to the buyer's mesh size, depth, twine thickness, and color specification — with the same production standard applied regardless of order size.
