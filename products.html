Reading the Spec Sheet

Three numbers that decide which net you need

MESH SIZE

The gap between knots

Measured side-to-side across one opening. Smaller mesh holds smaller catch but adds drag; larger mesh lets juveniles and bycatch pass through.

GSM

Grams per square metre

Net density and material weight. Higher GSM means a heavier, stronger net — better for cages and rough water, but harder to handle by hand.

PLY

Twine thickness

Number of strands twisted into each twine. More ply raises breaking strength for high-tension trawling or long-soak set nets.
