Reading the Spec Sheet

What the numbers on a net spec mean

DK / SK

Twine construction

DK and SK describe how the nylon strands are twisted into twine — thicker and multi-ply twine holds up to heavier loads and rougher handling.

MESH SIZE

The gap between knots

Measured side-to-side across one opening. Matched to the fish species and fishing zone you're working.

MD

Net depth

Measured in mesh-deep (MD) — the number of mesh rows from the top to bottom rope of the net panel.
