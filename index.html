How It's Made

From raw filament to finished net

STAGE 1

Filament extrusion

HDPE and nylon yarn extruded and drawn to target tenacity before twisting into twine.

STAGE 2

Mesh weaving

Raschel and knotting machines set the mesh gauge — from fine 12mm to wide 200mm openings.

STAGE 3

Tension testing

Each batch is pull-tested for breaking strength against the rated load before approval.

STAGE 4

Finishing & dispatch

Border roping, dyeing, and panel sizing to order, then packed for domestic or export transit.
