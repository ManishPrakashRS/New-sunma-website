Frequently Asked

Before you send a requirement

Can you make a custom mesh size, depth, or twine thickness?

Yes — mesh, depth, color, and thickness can be made based on your specification. Send your target spec and quantity and we'll confirm feasibility and lead time.

What's the minimum order quantity?

We supply from 1 Ton to 50+ Ton for distributors, wholesalers, Importer & Exporters. There's no fixed minimum — tell us your quantity and we'll quote accordingly.

How long does delivery take?

First order: 8–10 business days. Repeat orders: within 7 business days, for a fast reorder supply chain within India. And for overseas depending upon terms.

What are your payment terms?

70% advance upon order confirmation, 10% upon Invoicing & 20% balance payable upon shipment documents.

Do you supply outside India?

SUNMA Fishnet currently serves buyers across India and Globally. Export & Overseas supply can be discussed for future bulk requirements after proper documentation and buyer confirmation — reach out with your destination and quantity.
