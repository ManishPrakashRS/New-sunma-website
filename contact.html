Frequently Asked

Before you send a spec

Can you weave a custom mesh size or GSM?

Yes — most orders are woven to a specific mesh size, GSM, and panel dimension. Send your target spec and quantity and we'll confirm feasibility and lead time.

Do you ship outside India?

Yes, we export to fleets and aquaculture operations across multiple countries. Share your destination port and we'll quote shipping along with the product.

What's the minimum order quantity?

We supply everything from single panels for individual boats to full container loads for distributors. There's no fixed minimum — tell us your quantity and we'll quote accordingly.

How long does a custom order take?

Lead time depends on mesh size, quantity, and finishing requirements. Standard specs typically ship faster than fully custom panel dimensions — we'll confirm a timeline with your quote.
